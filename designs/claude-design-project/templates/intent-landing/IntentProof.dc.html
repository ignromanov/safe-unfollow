Rejected forms

  • Eight rows. Puts the button past 1,150px on desktop; on a 390px phone the card alone is ~700px. The count carries the proof, not row seven.
  • A results-screen replica (stat tiles + filter chips + list). Reads as the tool, and the tool is exactly what this page cannot be — the reader taps a chip, nothing happens, and the page has taught them it is a mock.
  • Hiding the remainder ("and more…"). The count printed must be the count rendered (§5); "26 more" is the honest version of an ellipsis.
  • Chips recoloured for AA. Drawn as they ship (GH#211). The 10px chip label is the smallest type on the page and the one place the palette defect is visible; see Q6.
